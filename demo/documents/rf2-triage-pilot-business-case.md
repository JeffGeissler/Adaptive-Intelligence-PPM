## Part A: Business case (2026-06-20)

**Problem.** Agents spend about 1.5 minutes per ticket reading and routing new tickets to the right queue.

**Proposal.** Turn on HelixDesk's AI triage add-on for one 30-agent queue for Q3 2026. The tool reads each new ticket and routes it automatically.

**Expected benefit.**
- 4,800 tickets in the pilot queue per quarter × 1.5 min saved = 7,200 min = **120 agent hours saved**.
- At a $25/hour loaded agent cost: **120 agent hours saved = $30,000 savings.**
- Scaling to all queues (about 4× the volume) would save "$120,000 per quarter."

**Cost.** AI triage add-on: $4,200 for the quarter. "Pays for itself many times over."

**Success criteria.** Hours saved ≥ 100 in Q3; misroute rate below 1%.

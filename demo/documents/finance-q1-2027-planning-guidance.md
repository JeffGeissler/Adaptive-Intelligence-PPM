## Benefit rules

| Benefit type | How Finance treats it |
|---|---|
| **Cash** | Counted only where a named budget line goes down (e.g., overtime, contract, or license spend). |
| **Avoided cost** | Future cost that would otherwise be incurred (e.g., penalties, planned hires). Not a budget reduction. |
| **Capacity** | Released staff hours. Valued at $25/hour loaded agent cost for comparison only. **Not cash unless headcount or hours are removed from the budget.** |
| **Revenue** | Incremental gross margin, not gross sales. |

- State confidence (High / Medium / Low) and the basis for every estimate.
- **Do not count the same benefit twice.** Where two initiatives claim the same contact reduction, show it once.
- Prior spend on an initiative is context only; evaluate cost to complete against alternatives.
